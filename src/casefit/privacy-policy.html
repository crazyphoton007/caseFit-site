Your trust matters to us. This policy explains what caseFit collects, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you across the app, website, consultations, payments, and support interactions.

The caseFit Company (“caseFit”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the caseFit mobile application and website located at https://thecasefit.com (collectively, the “Platform”). By accessing or using caseFit, you acknowledge that your information may be collected, used, stored, and shared as described in this Policy.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to information collected through the caseFit app, our website, our support channels, consultation booking flow, payment flow, and other features we may introduce from time to time. It covers both registered users and visitors who interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use caseFit, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and profile information: your name, mobile number, email address, gender, age, city or area, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Consultation and case information: the category of your legal issue, appointment preferences, notes you submit, and details shared during the request flow.
  • Payment information: payment status, transaction references, and billing-related metadata processed through trusted third-party payment partners such as Razorpay. We do not store your full card or bank details on our own systems.
  • Technical and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, browser information, IP address, session activity, diagnostic data, and interaction patterns.
  • Communications and feedback: messages sent to support, feedback you submit inside the app, and records of service-related communications.
  • Analytics and cookies: information collected through analytics, diagnostics, and performance tools used to improve reliability and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate caseFit responsibly and deliver the experience you expect.

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity, including OTP-based login.
  • To help you raise consultation requests, process bookings, and coordinate appointments.
  • To assign lawyers, share scheduling details, and maintain request history and case status updates.
  • To process payments, confirm transactions, prevent payment fraud, and support refunds where applicable.
  • To improve app performance, stability, service quality, design decisions, and customer support.
  • To communicate important service updates, confirmations, issue resolution, and support responses.
  • To detect abuse, misuse, suspicious activity, security incidents, and legal or policy violations.
  • To comply with applicable law, lawful requests, legal process, and internal risk management obligations.

4. AI and Automated Processing

caseFit may include AI-assisted features such as summaries, content suggestions, legal education material, or guided experiences. These tools are intended to support discovery and user experience. They do not replace independent legal advice, professional judgment, or a lawyer-client relationship.

5. Legal Basis and Consent

We process personal information where it is necessary to provide our services, fulfill our contractual obligations, maintain platform security, comply with legal obligations, or where you have consented to particular uses such as optional communications or support interactions.

6. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including access controls, authentication, encryption, logging, and role-based restrictions where appropriate. Your data may be stored on secure cloud infrastructure, including services hosted in India and/or other cloud regions used by our providers. We retain information only for as long as it is needed for service delivery, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security, audit requirements, or other legitimate business purposes. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to maintain commercially reasonable protections.

7. How We Share Information

We may share limited information where necessary with:

  • Lawyers or legal professionals connected to your consultation request.
  • Payment partners such as Razorpay for transaction processing and payment verification.
  • Cloud hosting, infrastructure, communications, and analytics vendors who support our services.
  • Service providers that help us with customer support, security monitoring, diagnostics, and product operations.
  • Regulators, law enforcement, courts, or government authorities when required by law or lawful order.
  • Professional advisers or acquirers in connection with business restructuring, financing, diligence, or transfer events.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not trade user data as a commercial asset.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and product availability, you may request to:

  • Access or update your profile and personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information associated with your account.
  • Request deletion of your account or certain personal data, subject to legal and operational limits.
  • Opt out of promotional communications while continuing to receive essential service-related updates.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is used or request additional support.

To exercise these rights or make a privacy-related request, email us at support@thecasefit.com.

9. OTP, Notifications, and Communications

We may use your mobile number, email address, or approved communication channels to send login codes, service confirmations, payment updates, appointment details, feedback acknowledgements, and essential account messages. Promotional communication, if introduced, will be managed separately and may be turned off where available.

10. Payment and Refund Information

Payments are processed through secure third-party gateways. Refunds, cancellations, and payment disputes are handled in accordance with our operational policies and applicable Terms & Conditions. We may retain limited transaction metadata for reconciliation, fraud prevention, compliance, and customer support.

11. Children's Privacy

caseFit is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the Platform, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Our app or website may contain links to third-party sites, payment gateways, court resources, or external tools. Those services operate under their own policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that are not controlled by caseFit.
